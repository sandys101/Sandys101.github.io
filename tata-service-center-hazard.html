I took the day off work. I waited.

I was handed a gate pass and sent on my way.

Two full days passed before I finally forced the dealership to produce a digital soft copy over WhatsApp (Consolidated Invoice(e) [Redacted].PDF). When the PDF opened, the actual mechanics of their billing system emerged.

04 The Paint Polish Double-Dip: Charging Twice for the Same Sheet Metal

The rear wiper was not an isolated clerical glitch. Under "Optional Services" on the exact same invoice, Phytron Mobility billed two distinct full-body paint treatments concurrently:

Item No. 17 · AKPAINTPR

UV PROTECTION POLISH RUB WITH SPECIAL COATING - AUTOKROM

₹2,006.00
Item No. 18 · 3MPAINTPR

UV PROTECTION POLISH RUB WITH SPECIAL COATING - 3M

That is ₹3,363.00 in redundant cosmetic packages. Did their technicians polish the car with Autokrom compound, buff it down, and immediately apply a competing 3M compound over it on the same afternoon? Or did a billing operator simply select every lucrative value-added package available on the dealership software?

While my formal query regarding the wiper fraud sat unanswered by dealership leadership, the dealership’s automated CRM desk ("Zedex Tata Kiran") initiated a relentless bombardment on WhatsApp:

06 The Terrifying Revelation: The Unrepaired Fuel Leak

Over the following weeks, the nightmare turned dangerous. Inside the cabin, a pungent chemical stench of evaporating petrol lingered every time the car sat in traffic or idled.

Assuming the newly installed pipe had a loose fitting or clamp, I drove the Nexon to a reputable multibrand service center for an independent physical inspection.

The technicians put the car on the hydraulic lift, stripped back the protective panels, and called me under the bay.

THE PHYSICAL EVIDENCE UNDER THE HYDRAULIC BAY

The petrol pipe was saturated, degraded, and weeping fuel. It was the original factory part. It had never been unbolted. It had never been replaced.

I reopened the official Tata invoice ([Invoice No. Redacted]) and examined every single entry from line 1 to line 19:
