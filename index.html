Welcome to my personal space. This website is a personal canvas, not a commercial portfolio. It is an intimate digital darkroom dedicated to a single, uncompromising vision: how I see the world through my Sony Alpha mirrorless camera, backed by 15 years of RAW image experimentation.

Here, I share my personal viewpoint in the form of articles, pictures, and videos—documenting unhurried stories from open roads, quiet late night hours, and everyday life.
